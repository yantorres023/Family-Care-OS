- Facebook: AARP Family Caregivers Discussion Group (~19k), The Caregiver Space (~8k), many "Caring for Elderly Parents" groups.
- Forums: AgingCare.com (active sibling-conflict threads).

- Search moments: "siblings not helping with aging parents", "caregiver log template", "how to split caregiving with siblings".

## Referral hypothesis
The shared update **is** the referral surface: every update posted to a family chat shows a small "Sent with Baton" footer (user can turn off). Hypothesis: ≥5% of recipients who see ≥3 updates search/install. Measurable only with store attribution once published.
