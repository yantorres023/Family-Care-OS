**Full description**

Caring for a parent with your siblings? Baton keeps everyone on the same page.

• One list of what needs doing — groceries, bills, rides, appointments, check-in calls.
• Every task has an owner, or clearly "needs someone".
• Today shows what's overdue, what's happening today, and what's coming up.
• Repeating tasks for weekly groceries or monthly bills.
• Leave a quick note for the next person: "Groceries delivered, prescription still to pick up."
• Send a clean update to your family group chat in one tap. Your family doesn't need to install anything.
• Using a shared tablet at Mom's house? Switch who's using Baton so everyone gets credit.
• A timeline of what happened and who did it.
• Gentle reminders for your own tasks, with private lock-screen text.

Private by design: no account, no ads, no trackers. Everything stays on your phone. Export or delete your data any time.

Baton is for organising family help. It is not a medical app and does not give medical advice. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
