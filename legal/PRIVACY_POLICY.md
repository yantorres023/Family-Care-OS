**Summary.** Baton is an organisation tool that helps families coordinate everyday care tasks for someone they look after. In this version, **everything you enter stays on your device.** We do not run a server for Baton, we do not require an account, and we do not receive your data.

## 1. What Baton stores on your device
- The name or nickname you give the person you care for.
- Names you enter for yourself and other helpers, and their roles in the app.
- Tasks and appointments (titles, dates, times, repeat settings, who is doing them, places, notes, "important" flags, completion history).
- Notes you write for the next person ("handoff notes").
- A history of changes (the Timeline).
- Your settings.
- Simple usage counts (for example, how many tasks were created), which never include text you typed.

Baton is **not designed to hold medical information.** Please do not enter diagnoses, medications, dosages or other health details.

## 2. What leaves your device
Nothing, unless you choose to share it:
- **Sharing an update or a help request** opens your phone's share sheet with the text shown to you. Whatever app you pick (for example WhatsApp or Messages) handles it under its own privacy policy. Task notes are never included in these updates.
- **Exporting data** creates a file you control.
- **Device backups:** your phone's operating system may include Baton's data in backups to your Apple or Google account, according to your device settings.

We do not use advertising, trackers, or third-party analytics SDKs, and we do not sell or share personal information.

## 3. Notifications
Reminders are scheduled on your device. By default they hide task names on the lock screen. You can change or turn them off in Settings or in your phone's settings.

## 4. Your choices and rights
- **Access / portability:** Settings → Export all data.
- **Deletion:** Settings → Delete all data, or uninstall the app.
- Because we do not hold your data, we cannot access, correct, or delete it for you; these controls are in the app.
- Depending on where you live (for example the UK/EU under UK GDPR/GDPR, California under CCPA/CPRA, Canada under PIPEDA), you have rights over personal information. [Legal review: confirm controller analysis for on-device-only processing.]

## 5. Children
Baton is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed to children.

## 6. Other people's information
You may enter other people's names (family members, the person you care for). Please only enter what is needed to coordinate, and let them know you are using Baton.

## 7. Changes
If a future version adds syncing between phones or any data collection, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required **before** anything is collected.

## 8. Not medical advice
Baton is for organisation and coordination. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
